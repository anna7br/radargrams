GPR survey: picking the glacier surface and bed

Inter-analyst comparison of manual reflection picking in UAV-borne ground-penetrating radar  •  ~15–25 min  •  runs in your browser

What this is

Thank you for taking part! We are measuring how consistently experts pick reflections in glacier GPR radargrams. On each image you draw two lines: the glacier surface and the bed. There are no graded "right" answers — we want your natural interpretation, so please pick as you normally would.

How to pick

  • Choose the horizon with the Surface (s) / Bed (b) buttons (or the s / b keys). Surface draws in cyan, bed in orange.
  • Surface: left-click along the air–glacier (or, under winter snow, snow–ice) reflection — the top of the ice.
  • Bed: left-click along the ice–bedrock reflection — the bottom of the ice. Where the ice lies above an air-filled cavity, follow the bottom of the ice (the ice–air interface inside the cavity), not the rock below.
  • Add points left to right. Zoom with the mouse wheel, pan by dragging with the right mouse button. "Undo point" (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) and "Clear horizon" act on the horizon you are currently picking.
  • If you cannot interpret the bed on an image, tick "bed not interpretable" (you can still pick the surface) and move on — please do not guess.
  • Move between images with Next / Prev (or the arrow keys); your lines are kept.

When you are finished

  1. Click Download my results (CSV) in the tool — this saves a file named analyst_YOURNAME.csv.
  2. Upload that file via the green button below (you will be asked to sign in to Google so the file is saved to our project drive).

Please

  • Work independently — do not discuss or compare picks with other participants.
  • Pick both horizons on every image you can.
  • Try to finish in one sitting: your work is held in the browser tab and is saved only when you click Download. Nothing is sent anywhere until you upload.
